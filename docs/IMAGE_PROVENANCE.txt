WAFFRLI DEMO MEDIA — IMAGE PROVENANCE

This bundle contains synthetic demo imagery made for testing mock restaurant and product listings.
- The sixteen food pictures originate from an AI-generated 4 x 4 photographic food showcase, cropped into separate assets.
- The remaining non-food mock product assets are original vector-style product illustrations rendered to JPG with Python/Pillow.
- No authentic restaurant-brand logos or official medical-product labels are used.
- The source media is illustrative only, not evidence that any named demo store is real.
- Do not use these materials to impersonate an existing company or claim that fictional shops are real.

Images ship locally; runtime does not require Unsplash, Pexels, a remote CDN, or any third-party API.
